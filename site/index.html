DataDome: from a customer's fraud ticket to a better detection signal

How a Fraud Analyst goes from a customer's ticket ("our accounts are being stolen") to the fraud pattern, a clear report and a new detection signal tested offline. Real public data (31.3 M synthesized logins, 67,352 labelled web sessions, published crawler lists) and a simulated platform with 12 fictional customers, labelled on every view.
